## Finishing

- **Fringe** — price varies depending on finishing options and warp fibre

  - Twisted fringe with hemstitch — $75–120 per side
- **Lace Detail** — $40+ per row (multiple options available at different price points)
- **Inlay and color blocking details** — discussed and billed on a case-by-case basis

## Returning Customer Discount

10% discount after purchase of a wrap piece (3 meters +) from a custom, semi-custom, or ready-to-ship warp. Discount is only applicable after purchase of a wrap piece 3 meters + and can be used toward purchases including pre-orders and accessories. Discount not applicable on auctions or reduced-price/clearance merchandise.

I will remove the discount from invoice if payment is not made as agreed upon in a payment plan, and no communication is made toward changes to payment plans. If you qualify for this discount and wish to purchase something from the website, please PM me for a code.
