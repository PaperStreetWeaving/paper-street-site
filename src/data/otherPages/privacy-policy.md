_Last updated: May 2026_

Paper Street Weaving is a one-person small business run by Deidre Cleator. This privacy policy explains what information I collect through this website and how I use it.

## What I collect

This website does not require accounts or logins. I do not run analytics that track individual visitors. The only personal information I collect is what you share with me directly when you email me about a custom order — typically your name, email address, shipping address, and details about the piece you're ordering.

## How I use it

I use the information you share with me to:

- Communicate with you about your order
- Quote, invoice, and ship your piece
- Keep records as required for Canadian tax purposes

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone except as required to fulfill your order (e.g., shipping carriers).

## Cookies

This website does not set tracking cookies. Some hosting infrastructure may set technical cookies necessary for the site to function — none of these are used to track you across other sites.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Ask what information I have about you
- Ask me to correct or delete your information
- Ask me to stop using your information

To make any of these requests, email **paperstreetfibrearts@gmail.com**.

## Changes

If I update this policy, I'll change the "last updated" date above.
